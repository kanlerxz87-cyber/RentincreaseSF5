## 2. State — California

| Statute | Verified content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Civil Code § 827 | Written notice: 30 days for ≤10%; 90 days when the increase alone or combined in 12 months >10%; personal delivery or mail per CCP 1013 | [leginfo](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=827&lawCode=CIV) |
| Civil Code § 1947.12 (AB 1482) | 5% + CPI or 10% whichever lower; **(d)(3)** exempts housing already subject to a local ordinance capping increases below 5% + CPI; **(d)(5)(A)** SFH/condo exemption unavailable to REITs, corporations, LLCs with a corporate member; **(e)** notice per § 827 | [leginfo](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1947.12.&lawCode=CIV), [sf.gov AB 1482](https://www.sf.gov/reports--california-tenant-protection-act-2019-ab-1482) |
| AB 1482 rate in SF | 8.8% for 8/1/2026–7/31/2027, but "This does NOT apply for units under the rent increase limitations of the San Francisco Rent Ordinance" | [sf.gov AB 1482](https://www.sf.gov/reports--california-tenant-protection-act-2019-ab-1482) |
| Civil Code § 1954.53 (Costa-Hawkins) | Owner may set initial rent for a new tenancy, except where the prior tenancy was terminated by a § 1946.1 notice or a change in terms noticed under § 827, and other exceptions | [leginfo](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1954.53.&lawCode=CIV) |
| Government Code § 12955 | Unlawful for a housing owner to discriminate/harass because of race, color, religion, sex, gender, gender identity/expression, sexual orientation, marital status, national origin, ancestry, familial status, source of income, disability, veteran/military status, genetic information; **(f)** retaliation | [leginfo](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=12955.&lawCode=GOV) |

## 4. Zip code 94122
