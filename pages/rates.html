Allowable Annual Rent Increases — San Francisco

Current rate: 1.6% (3/1/2026 – 2/28/2027) · Formula: 60% of Bay Area CPI-U · Statutory cap: 7% · Citywide rate (not ZIP-specific)

How the rate is set

Legal basis: SF Admin Code Sec. 37.3(a)(1): on March 1 of each year the Rent Board publishes the increase in the CPI for the preceding 12 months (U.S. Department of Labor). A landlord who has reported the unit per Sec. 37.15 has a license to impose an annual increase of up to 60% of the published CPI increase. In no event may the allowable annual increase exceed 7%.

Current rate: 1.6% effective March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027 (announced January 14, 2026). Prior period: 1.4% (3/1/2025–2/28/2026).

Sources: Sec. 37.3 — Rent Limitations · Annual rent increase announcement · Learn about rent increases
