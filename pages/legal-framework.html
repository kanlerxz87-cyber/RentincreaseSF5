L11Costa-HawkinsCivil Code 1954.50 et seq. allows an owner to set the initial rent for a new tenancy, with exceptions1954.53(a)(1): exception where the previous tenancy was terminated by a 1946.1 notice or a change in terms noticed under 827. SF implements this in Sec. 37.3(d) and Rule 6.14. No market-rate increase while an original occupant remains.VerifiedS32S13S20
L12Tenant Protection Act (AB 1482)5% + CPI or 10%, whichever is lower; 8.8% in San Francisco for 8/1/2026-7/31/2027 - but NOT applicable to SF rent-controlled unitsCivil Code 1947.12(d)(3) exempts units already subject to a local ordinance capping increases below 5% + CPI. SF Rent Board: 'This does NOT apply for units under the rent increase limitations of the San Francisco Rent Ordinance.'VerifiedS31S27S03
L13NoticeCivil Code 827: 30 days for <=10%; 90 days if >10% alone or combined within 12 months; mail service per CCP 1013Applies to both rent-controlled and non-rent-controlled units. sf.gov adds 5 days when mailed.VerifiedS30S01S31
