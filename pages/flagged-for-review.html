F01HighConflicting official rules on when a skipped annual increase becomes 'banked'Rule 4.12(a) says 'A full 12 months must have elapsed'; the current sf.gov 'Banked rent increases' page says 'A skipped increase is only banked if at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase'. Both are official SF Rent Board sources. The worked examples on sf.gov 'Learn about rent increases' and 'Increasing rents during irregular periods' follow the 12-month logic.Changes Unit 2's maximum lawful increase from 13.1% to 11.7% and changes whether the June 1, 2025 1.4% is preserved. Do not serve a banked-increase notice before this is resolved.Ask a Rent Board counselor for the current interpretation and have a California tenant-side attorney review the calculation.
