What is a "reasonable and normal" increase? Under SF rent control, the normal increase for a continuing tenancy is the annual allowable increase — currently 1.6% (March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027) — imposed once every 12 months on the tenant's anniversary date, without a petition. Skipped prior increases may be added as banked amounts. Anything above that requires a Rent Board petition and approval. A normal increase is therefore: annual allowable (1.6%) + any lawful banked amount, calculated on base rent, with no rounding up. Learn about rent increases · Banked increases

Rent history documentation

Before any increase, assemble the complete rent history for each unit (lease start date, every rent and increase date, and any board determinations). The Rent Board can provide a determination of the tenant's current lawful rent, and tenants may request a review of their rent history. A complete history is required for any petition based on past rent history and protects against unlawful-increase claims.
