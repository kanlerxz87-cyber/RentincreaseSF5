U10Can the April 1, 2026 occupant change reset the rent to market?NO - an original occupant still resides in the unitSec. 37.3(d)(2)(B) excludes a 'partial change[] in occupancy ... where one or more of the occupants ... remains an occupant in lawful possession'. Rule 6.14 / Costa-Hawkins market-rate increases require ALL original occupants to have vacated. Sec. 37.3(a)(11)(A) separately bars any increase imposed solely because an occupant was added. sf.gov: 'Landlords in San Francisco cannot charge more rent simply because a new roommate ... has moved into a unit.'VerifiedS13S20S19S16S01

Civil Code 1954.53(a)(1) is consistent.VerifiedS13S32
