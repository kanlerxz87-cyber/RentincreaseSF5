Fair housing and the one-unit vs both-units question

What is lawful, what is not, and how to document a unit-neutral decision.

Verified 2026-11-07 · UTC

One unit or both?

Raising only one unit is not inherently unlawful, and no official source requires raising both. The Rent Ordinance is tenancy-specific: different lease dates, base rents, anniversary dates and banked histories legitimately produce different amounts. What is unlawful is choosing the unit or the amount because of a protected characteristic or in retaliation. Adopt one written, uniformly applied, unit-neutral policy and document the objective reason for each amount. S01S12S10S33S35S28
