U36What is 'reasonable and normal' under official sourcesThe published annual allowable increase (1.6% for 3/1/2026-2/28/2027), applied once per 12 months on base rent, with a current license and correct written noticeANALYSIS: this is the only increase that official sources describe as available without a petition or approval, and it is the same for every covered unit in the City regardless of ZIP code.
