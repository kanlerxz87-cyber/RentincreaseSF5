Official allowable annual increases (Form 571)

The complete official rate table and how the current 1.6% rate is applied.

Verified 2026-11-07 · UTC

Current allowable annual increase: 1.6%

Effective March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027, for rent-controlled units. Based on 60% of the increase in CPI-U for the Bay Area; statutory ceiling 7%; once every 12 months; calculated on base rent only; no rounding up. S02S04S01S03S13

Use the percentage in effect on the effective date of the increase, not the date the notice is served. S01 The 2027–2028 rate has not been published; its cell below is intentionally blank and flagged. S02S42S46

How the rate is used

IDTopic / requirementValue / findingDetail / verificationStatusOfficial source(s)
R08FrequencyOnly ONE rent increase per 12 months for a tenancyFirst increase no earlier than 12 months after the lease began; after an increase, its effective date becomes the anniversary date.VerifiedS01S12S15
R09AnniversaryUse the tenant's anniversary date; a later effective date creates a NEW anniversary dateIf the increase is imposed more than 12 months later, the effective date becomes the new anniversary date and the owner must wait another 12 months. Moving the date earlier can permanently lose an available increase.VerifiedS01S12
R10AmountUse the allowable percentage in effect on the effective date (currently 1.6%)Not the percentage in effect on the date the notice is served.VerifiedS01S02S04
R11AmountCalculate on BASE RENT onlyBase rent includes housing services such as parking/storage but excludes capital improvement increases (37.7), utility passthroughs, water revenue bond passthroughs, and general obligation bond passthroughs.VerifiedS01S14S11
R12AmountNever round up to the nearest dollarStated on sf.gov and in Rule 4.12(a) for banked increases.VerifiedS01S15S11
R13BankingAdd skipped percentages together; do NOT compound or prorateBanked increases need no Rent Board approval but must be calculated correctly. No cap on total banked; they do not expire during a tenancy; they do NOT carry over to a new tenant; only increases available on/after April 1, 1982 count.VerifiedS10S15S11
