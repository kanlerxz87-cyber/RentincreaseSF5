The Rent Board process, step by step

What has to be filed, what does not have to be filed, and in what order — from confirming coverage to serving a notice that survives a petition.

Answer first: licence yes, approval no

The confusion comes from one word doing two jobs. San Francisco uses “licence” for the permission the ordinance grants an owner who has complied with Housing Inventory reporting. It is not a permit you apply for, and no official approves the amount.

What you file: the annual Housing Inventory report in the Rent Board Portal, under penalty of perjury — owner or manager business contact, unit size, bedrooms, bathrooms, the date occupancy began, and the base rent in $250 increments S3 S4.

What you get: a rent increase licence, generated automatically for a non-owner-occupied unit S3. It does not have to be attached to your notice, but it must exist and be current on the effective date of the notice S3.

What you never file for a normal increase: an application, an approval request, or a copy of the notice S2 S9.

The sequence

1

Confirm the unit is covered

Pre-13 June 1979, two or more residential units, not a single-family dwelling or condominium. A house with a legal or illegal in-law unit counts as a two-unit building and is not exempt S12 S13. A 1940 two-unit building sits squarely inside the ordinance.

Output: written confirmation of coverage, kept with the file.
2

Report into the Housing Inventory and capture the licence

Free, online, 24/7, due 1 March every year. For condominiums and buildings with fewer than 10 units, reporting began 1 March 2023 S3 S4. Late filing is accepted and there is no monetary penalty, but a notice is not enforceable without a licence on its effective date S3. Report changes to owner or manager business contact within 30 days S4.

Output: licence status confirmed by date; portal record or screenshot retained.
3

Fix the base rent and the anniversary date

Base rent is the rent charged at initial occupancy plus every allowable increase actually imposed S6. The first annual increase may be imposed 12 months after the lease began, and each increase date then becomes the anniversary date S1.

Output: one line per unit — base rent, start date, anniversary date, date of last increase.
4

Compute the allowable amount from the official table

Add the official percentages for each available year; do not compound and do not round up S9 S2. The annual portion is the percentage in effect on the effective date of the increase, not the date it is served S1.

Output: percentage and dollar figure with the source year for every component.
5

Re-check the licence against the planned effective date

If the inventory report will not be substantially compliant by that date, move the date. During a suspension the tenant is not obligated to pay the increased rent S4 S3.

Output: an effective date that falls after the licence date.
6

Draft the notice with every required disclosure

Dollar amount, percentage and effective date S1; which portion is the annual increase and which is banked S5 S9; and, for a banked amount, the years it comes from S11. Any other component must be identified separately S5.

Output: notice drafted from a checklist. See Notices.
7

Serve it with enough lead time

30 days for 10% or less; 90 days for more than 10%; five extra days if mailed S1 S16. Delivery is personal, or by mail under Code of Civil Procedure §1013 S16. Keep proof of service.

Output: dated proof of service and a copy per tenant.
8

Update the record and the cycle

The new amount becomes the base rent and the effective date becomes the anniversary date; the next increase cannot be given for 12 months S1 S6. Update the rent figure in the next March 1 Housing Inventory report.

Output: rent ledger updated with the notice copy attached.
9

If the tenant petitions

A tenant may file Form C at any time, free, alleging an unlawful increase or seeking a determination of the lawful rent S20. It goes to an arbitration hearing before a Rent Board Administrative Law Judge, with the decision appealable to the Rent Board Commission S20 S18. A non-conforming increase is null and void S5 S9.

Output: the file built in steps 2–7 is the defence.
