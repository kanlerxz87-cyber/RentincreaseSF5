## 2. Licence question — verified verbatim (comes first)

§37.15(a) requires **every** owner of a residential unit subject to Chapter 37 to report; there
is **no** entity-form or portfolio-size threshold, so an individual owner is covered exactly like
a corporation. Reporting a tenant-occupied unit into the Housing Inventory **generates the
rent-increase licence automatically** — there is no application form and no approval to wait
for. The licence must be current **on the increase's effective date**; suspension is prospective
only and the tenant owes no increased rent for suspended months. No petition is needed for an
ordinary annual or properly banked increase; the multiple-year guidance and Form 571 both state
these increases are imposed without filing a petition.

**Conclusion:** an individual owner must hold the licence, but there is no separate application.
Report into the Inventory first, confirm the licence is on file, then serve notice. This is the
first section of the published executive summary and the first panel of the landing page.
