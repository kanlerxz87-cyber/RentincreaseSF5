Tenant-lawyer stress test

Review the substance, timing and motivation—not just the percentage.

Question / checkpointFindingEvidence
Can a lawyer still challenge it?Yes. A license does not validate the ledger, coverage, lease rights, notice, service or motive. No attorney has reviewed these units and no “lawyer-proof” result is promised.Housing Inventory & license ↗ · Civil Code §1942.5 ↗ · Civil Code §827 ↗
Selective increase — analysisAn increase for only one unit is not automatically discriminatory. Document objective lease/timing/licensing reasons and apply a neutral policy. Do not choose a unit because of a protected characteristic or protected activity.California CRD housing rights ↗ · Civil Code §1942.5 ↗ · Rent increase guide ↗
Both units — analysisThe same percentage when each is eligible can improve consistency, but it is not a legal immunity. Same dates may be inappropriate if a lease or 12-month interval prevents one increase. Different base rents naturally yield different dollars.Rent increase guide ↗ · California CRD housing rights ↗
Retaliation red flag§1942.5 restricts retaliatory increases, including specified tenantability-related activity within its 180-day framework and lawful organizing/exercise of rights. Do not treat day 181 as automatic immunity. Review the full statute and facts.Civil Code §1942.5 ↗
Fair housing red flagNo higher rent based on protected characteristics, including California source-of-income protections. Do not assume small-owner status eliminates all applicable discrimination protections; exemptions require individualized review.California CRD housing rights ↗ · HUD fair housing enforcement ↗
Repair/services red flagDo not disguise reduced services or neglect with an otherwise allowable percentage. Review outstanding repairs and tenant claims independently; the annual allowance is not a release from other legal duties.Civil Code §1942.5 ↗ · Rent increase guide ↗
Free tenant counselSF’s official notice describes free assistance through Tenant Right to Counsel when eviction summons/complaint is received. This is not evidence that every routine rent-increase dispute automatically receives a free personal lawyer.SF tenant right-to-counsel notice ↗
Suggested explanation — not a legal notice“This is an annual allowable rent adjustment under the SF Rent Ordinance, not an additional-occupant charge. The notice identifies the percentage, dollar change and effective date.” Use only if accurate and accompanied by compliant notice/service.Rent increase guide ↗ · Rent Board Rules, Part IV ↗
