RENT-CONTROLLED HOUSING

Executive summary

Start here. License first, then timing, calculation and notice.

THE FIRST QUESTION

Yes. Individual owners need the Rent Board license too.

The Housing Inventory requirement is not limited to corporations. Reporting a tenant-occupied unit automatically generates its rent increase license. There is no separate license application described in the official process.

Legal timing: a current license must be on file when the annual or banked increase takes effect—not necessarily before you serve the notice. Recommended sequence: report and confirm the license first, then serve notice. The license need not be attached, and is not a guarantee against a challenge.
