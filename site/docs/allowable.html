How the number is set

The Rent Board publishes one percentage a year, effective March 1. It is 60% of the increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers in the Bay Area, rounded to the nearest tenth, and it can never exceed 7%. San Francisco Rent Ordinance ↗ San Francisco Rent Board ↗

To turn it into dollars: base rent × 0.016. The Rent Board's own example: a $2,000 base rent rises by $32.00 to $2,032.00. San Francisco Rent Board ↗

  • Base rent only. Includes housing services such as parking or storage; excludes temporary passthroughs and fluctuating charges. San Francisco Rent Board ↗
  • Do not round up. San Francisco Rent Board ↗
  • Once every 12 months. The first increase may be imposed 12 months after the lease began; after that, the effective date becomes the anniversary date. San Francisco Rent Board ↗
  • Use the rate in effect on the effective date, not the date you serve the notice. San Francisco Rent Board ↗

Why the 2019–2020 rate of 2.6% never appears in Unit B's maths Unit B's tenancy began June 1, 2019, so its first increase eligibility date was June 1, 2020 — which falls in the 3/1/2020–2/28/2021 period (1.8%).
