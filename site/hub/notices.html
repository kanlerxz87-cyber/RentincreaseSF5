Notices

The notice is where most increases fail. The period, the content and the method of service are all prescribed.

R45Tenant route: defer an increase while repairs are outstanding (Form 516B)
A tenant may petition to defer an annual increase if required repairs were not made. The petition must be filed no later than 60 days after the tenant receives the rent increase notice; a granted petition postpones the increase until the work is done, and the anniversary date is not affected.

Reasons for the increase

An annual or banked increase does not need a stated justification - the ordinance authorises it and the notice must instead identify the annual and banked portions. S3 S7 Reasons and cost evidence belong to petition-based increases such as certified capital improvements. S3 In practice a short, factual covering note helps: it should refer to the published annual percentage and the anniversary date, and should avoid any reference to a tenant's personal circumstances, a request for repairs, or a wish to see the unit vacated. Those are the facts that turn a lawful number into a retaliation or harassment claim. S17 S15

A model notice you can work from

This is a drafting aid for your own file. It is not a Rent Board form and it is not legal advice. Everything in square brackets is yours to complete accurately, and any line that does not apply to the unit should be deleted rather than guessed. The itemisation follows sec. 37.3(b); the 30/90-day periods follow Civil Code sec. 827(b). S3 S8

NOTICE OF RENT INCREASE

Date of this notice:        [date you deliver or mail it]
To:                         [tenant name or names as they appear on the rental agreement]
Rental unit:                [unit identifier - do not put the full street address on a public site]
Current base rent:          $[amount] per month
New base rent:              $[amount] per month
Amount of the increase:     $[amount] per month
Percentage increase:        [x.x]% of the current base rent
Effective date:             [at least 30 days after this notice, or at least 90 days if the
                            increase is more than 10 percent; add 5 days if this is mailed]

How the increase is made up (Rent Ordinance sec. 37.3(b); Rules and Regulations sec. 4.10(a)):
    - Annual allowable increase:  [x.x]%, being the Rent Board allowance in effect on the
      effective date
    - Banked increase:            [x.x]%, based on the rent increase anniversary dates of
      [list the dates]

Rent increase licence: this unit is reported in the Rent Board Housing Inventory and a rent
increase licence is on file. The licence does not need to be attached to this notice.

Questions: Rent Board counsellors, 415-252-4600, weekdays 9am to noon and 1pm to 4pm.

Served by: [personal delivery / first-class mail] on [date]

Deliberately absent: any reason, any reference to a tenant's personal circumstances, and any occupant-based charge. There is no requirement to state a reason for an annual increase, and an occupant-based increase is prohibited. S3 S17
