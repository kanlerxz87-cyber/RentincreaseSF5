What this pass changed

  • F04 was rewritten. The earlier text described the 12-month and 24-month wording as reconciled; on a closer read the 24-month sentence cannot be squared with either worked example, so the time-stamped examples and Rule 4.12 are treated as controlling and the conflict is published rather than smoothed over.
  • The Unit 1 anniversary fork was added as F17. The answer changes if no original lessee remained after the 2026 occupant change, and that fact was not supplied.
  • A second limit was added: the complete Rules and Regulations compilation was linked but not read end to end (F19).
  • The rent notice kit was added, because the notice content rules are spread across three documents and are easy to get wrong in practice.

What this audit does not establish

  • No lease, rent ledger, portal record or property record was examined, so nothing here certifies that a particular increase is lawful on these facts.
  • The build year, the unit count and both base rents remain owner-supplied (F01, F02).
  • External links are checked for publisher and shape at build time, not for perpetual availability.
  • No browser rendering test was run; the HTML is validated by parser checks instead.
  • Nothing here is legal advice, and no notice, petition or licence action was taken.
