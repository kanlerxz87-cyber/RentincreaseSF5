The Rent Board process, step by step

What is filed, where, and in what order. An ordinary annual or banked increase needs no petition and no approval - but it does need the licence and a compliant notice.

  1. Confirm the unit is covered

    Units built on or before 13 June 1979 generally have rent control and eviction protection. Check the construction year on the Planning Department's Property Information Map, and speak to a Rent Board counsellor if the classification is unclear. S11

  2. Report into the Housing Inventory

    This is the step that replaces the 'application' you read about. Report each unit through the Rent Board Portal. It is free, online and available around the clock. For a building with fewer than 10 units the requirement began 1 March 2023 and repeats annually by 1 March. S1 S2

  3. Receive the rent increase licence automatically

    Reporting a unit as non-owner-occupied generates the licence. There is nothing to apply for and no approval decision to await. Tenants can see your licence status in the public portal. S1

  4. Pay the separate Rent Board fee

    $59.00 per dwelling unit for 2026-2027, due 1 March. A fee exemption does not satisfy the Inventory requirement, and the Inventory does not request a fee exemption. They are two separate obligations. S12 S1

  5. Calculate the increase

    Use the percentage in effect on the effective date, applied to base rent only. Add any banked percentages - added, never compounded, never rounded up. Identify which portion is banked and the dates it comes from. S4 S5 S7

  6. Check whether a petition is needed

    For an annual or banked increase, no petition. A petition is required only for the special types such as certified capital improvements or an operating and maintenance shortfall, and those must be filed before the notice. S3 S13

  7. Work out the notice period

    30 days if the increase is 10% or less, 90 days if it is more than 10%, measured against the rent charged in the previous 12 months and combined with any other increase in that window. Add 5 days if you serve by mail. S8 S5 S6

  8. Serve a compliant written notice

    State the dollar amount, the percentage, the effective date and the banked portion. Serve personally or by mail under Code of Civil Procedure sec. 1013. A non-conforming increase is null and void under Rule 4.10(b). S5 S7 S8

  9. Keep the evidence file

    Lease, every notice, proof of service, rent receipts and the calculation. A tenant can file Form 516C at no charge and with no time limit, so the file is your answer. S20

Widely repeated online claims of a licence 'application' are not supported by the official page.
No application. The licence is generated automatically once the unit is reported as non-owner-occupied in the Housing Inventory.VerifiedS1
R03Must the licence exist before the increase takes effect?Yes. A current rent increase licence must be on file before any annual or banked increase goes into effect; without it the notice is not enforceable.VerifiedS1 S2
R04What happens if the owner has not reported?
Sec. 37.15(e). This is not retroactive - late reporting does not revive past months.
