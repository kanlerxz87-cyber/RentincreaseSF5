Pending: a 10% cap on banked increases (File No. 261020)

Found on 10 November 2026. The Rent Board posted a notice about this proposal on 9 November 2026, after the earlier research on this site was compiled. It is not law. It is the single item most likely to change what you serve on Unit 2.

What the Rent Board posted

The same notice now appears on the Rent Board's rent increase page, its banked increases page, its multiple-year page, its current rates page and its forms centre: the Board of Supervisors is considering legislation that would temporarily limit the combined amount of banked rent increases and capital improvement rent increases to 10% per year. S5 S6 S26

The notice says the limit would apply to banked increases noticed on or after 15 September 2026, and it states in bold that the legislation has not been enacted and is subject to change. S6 S24

Today is 10 November 2026. A notice served now sits inside the window the proposal describes, which is why it matters even though nothing has passed.

The proposed text, as introduced

“A landlord may not impose banked rent increases under Section 37.3(a)(2), and/or rent increases based on any of the types of capital improvements described in Section 37.3(a)(3), that cause a tenant’s rent to increase by more than a total of 10% of the lowest base rent that was in effect at any time during the 12 months immediately preceding the date the increase takes effect, notwithstanding any other provision of this Chapter 37.”

Proposed new Sec. 37.3(h), File No. 261020, text as introduced. S25

What is not settled

The ordinance text caps increases imposed under Sections 37.3(a)(2)-(3) – banking and capital improvements. The ordinary annual increase lives in 37.3(a)(1), so on the face of the text it is outside the cap; the Rent Board’s plain-language summary simply says the cap is on “the combined amount of banked rent increases and capital improvement rent increases”. Neither source says what happens when an annual increase is served together with banked amounts. S25 S5 That gap is left blank in the register and published as F22 rather than resolved by inference.

The committee has not acted, no amendments are posted, and the Board has not voted. Status, amendments and any final text are on the official file. S24

What this means for the decision

  1. Treat 10% as the practical ceiling for Unit 2 while this is pending

    A 10% increase is lawful under current law and would remain lawful if the proposal is enacted as drafted. It also keeps the notice in the 30-day band. S8 S25

  2. Price the risk before choosing 13.1%

    If the ordinance passes as drafted after a 13.1% notice is served, the increase would be reached retroactively, and a non-conforming increase is challengeable with no filing fee and no time limit. S25 S20

  3. Re-check the file immediately before serving

    The status page is the authoritative record of what has been enacted. Nothing on this site should be relied on for the state of the legislation on the day you serve. S24

  4. Nothing here changes Unit 1

    Unit 1's figures stand as published; the proposal does not reach them. S25
