Model test against the Rent Board's own worked examples

If the banking model is right it must reproduce the Rent Board's published examples. The per-increase 12-month test (Rule 4.12(a)) matches all four. A loose reading of the banked page's 24-month sentence (bank every skipped anniversary once 24 months have passed) fails on EX-3, where the Rent Board says the 1.4% is lost after 35 months without an increase. That is why the "12 vs 24 months" item is resolved rather than open (audit finding A-03).
