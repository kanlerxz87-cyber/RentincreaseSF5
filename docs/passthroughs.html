Rent Board fee recoveryNone Once the fee is paid in full you may recover 50% ($29.50 per dwelling unit for 2026–27) from tenants in occupancy as of November 1 of the tax year. Cannot be evicted for non-payment.San Francisco Rent Board ↗
Excess water use chargesWorksheet Up to 50% of SF Water Department excess-use penalties, with retrofit certifications. Not part of base rent.San Francisco Rent Board ↗
Passthroughs do not become part of base rent Capital improvement amounts, utility passthroughs and water penalties are charged on top of base rent and are excluded from it when calculating future percentage increases. That is set out in Rule 4.11(h) and Rule 4.13(f), and in the definition of base rent. Getting this wrong compounds an overcharge every year. San Francisco Rent Board ↗
