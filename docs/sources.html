Treble damages and attorney's fees.

Verification method

  • Every percentage was transcribed from the official Form 571 PDF and then cross-checked against the Rent Board's “Current Rates” page.
  • The calculator (tools/calc.py) runs 21 automated self-tests on every build, reproducing the Rent Board's own published worked examples. All 21 pass. View the tests ↗
  • Dates are computed with real calendar arithmetic, not estimates.
  • Where the Rent Board has not published a figure, the field is rendered blank and routed to the Flagged for review page.
