How the layers interact

  • Local binds hardest. San Francisco's 1.6% is lower than the state cap, so the local number is the operative ceiling, and the local licence condition has no state equivalent.
  • State fills the gaps the ordinance leaves. Service method, the 10%/90-day notice split and the emergency price-gouging ceiling come from the Civil Code and Penal Code, not from Chapter 37.
  • Federal sets conduct, not price. The Fair Housing Act governs who you may discriminate against; it does not cap rent.
