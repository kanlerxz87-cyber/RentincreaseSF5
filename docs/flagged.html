2. Property type not verified

The building dates from 1940, which clears the June 13, 1979 cutoff. But that test is necessary, not sufficient. If either unit is a single-family home or a condominium, Costa-Hawkins removes the rent-increase limits entirely for tenancies that commenced on or after January 1, 1996 — which both of these did (2025 and 2019). The units would keep just-cause eviction protection and would still owe the Rent Board fee, but the 1.6% cap would not apply.

Two exceptions that would keep you covered: a single-family dwelling with a legal or illegal in-law unit is a two-unit building and is not exempt; and a single-family dwelling is not exempt if there is another residential structure on the same lot.

Action: confirm on the SF Property Information Map, or ask a Rent Board counselor.

4. Does fee delinquency suspend the license?

The Rent Board's pages tie the rent increase license to Housing Inventory reporting (§37.15(e)). None of the official pages reviewed state whether being delinquent on the Rent Board fee also suspends the license. They are described as separate obligations with separate deadlines, and the Rent Board warns that “requesting a Rent Board Fee exemption does not satisfy Rent Board Housing Inventory reporting requirements” — which is the reverse direction.

Action: pay the fee on time. Do not test the question.

5. Rent Board worked example applies an inconsistent rate

On sf.gov's “Learn about rent increases” page, the worked example titled “Rent increase for multiple years with new anniversary date” applies 1.6% to an increase effective May 1, 2026. The same page instructs landlords to “use the percentage that will be in effect on the date of the increase,” and 1.6% only took effect on March 1, 2026 — a May 1, 2026 effective date sits in the 1.4% period. The page's other examples follow the stated rule correctly.

Impact on you: none. Both of your units' effective dates are exactly on their anniversary dates, where the two readings agree. Noted for completeness and because it is the kind of thing to raise with a counselor if you ever move an anniversary date.
