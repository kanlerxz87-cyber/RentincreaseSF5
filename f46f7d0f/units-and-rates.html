Unit A analysis - continuing tenancy, so no market reset

Intake: lease start 2025-02-01 at $3,995.00 per month with three initial occupants; a continuing-tenancy occupancy change was reported for 2026-04-01 at an unchanged $3,995.00, leaving one original occupant (placeholder Tenant A1) plus one subsequent occupant (Tenant A4). Consequences from the official texts:

  • No surcharge may be charged merely because a new roommate joined the existing tenant - the City calls that an unlawful increase, even where a lease purports to allow it (S03).
  • While an original occupant still resides in the unit, a Costa-Hawkins vacancy reset to market is not available; subtenant and subsequent-occupant treatment follows Rule 6.14, which requires written notice within a reasonable time after the owner learns of the change (the 2026-04-01 notice status is unverified - FR-06) (S12, S22).
  • First annual increase was imposable on 2026-02-01 (the 1.4% period). Because it was skipped, it banks after 12 full months - on 2027-02-01 (S05).

Unit B analysis - long tenancy with potential banking

Intake: lease start 2019-06-01 at $3,150.00 per month with two occupants (placeholders Tenant B1 and Tenant B2). If - and only if - no annual increase was imposed from June 2020 onward (FR-05), the skipped allowances bank after 12 full months each and remain available because the tenancy continues; banking carries across owners and expires only when the tenancy ends (S05). The banked stack then totals 11.5% through the 2025-26 rate year (see composition table below). The City confirms banked portions are added, never compounded, and must be itemized with dates in the notice; a notice that fails Rule 4.10 is void.
