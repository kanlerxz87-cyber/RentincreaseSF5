Method

Twenty-five official sources were fetched and read on 2026-11-11 (San Francisco Rent Board and legislative pages, the California Legislature’s codes, the California Civil Rights Department, and the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development). Three load-bearing sources - the Sec. 37.15 text, the rent-increase guide with the File No. 261020 notice, and the Rent Board Fee page - were re-fetched a second time after the session rebased, to confirm nothing moved mid-day. Form 571 was re-fetched in full to transcribe every period. A source link beside a finding means the rule was read there on 2026-11-11; it never proves an owner-supplied property fact.

Legislative-record pages hosted on the City’s legislation portal (outside the official-source link allowlist used by this repository’s audit) are cited textually only: File No. 261020, record 8217304, introduced 2026-09-15, pending committee action as of 2026-11-11.
