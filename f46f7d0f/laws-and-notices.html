What a legally clean notice contains - and what counts as a reason

The ordinance and the statute care about the number, dates and service, not the owner’s justification: no cited rule conditions an annual or banked increase within the allowance on a stated reason. A truthful, neutral explanation (inflation, insurance and maintenance cost movements) is nonetheless good practice and helps a tenant-side reviewer see routine business purpose; it never authorizes exceeding the allowance, and invented reasons can create harassment exposure under Sec. 37.10B (S10).
