Review method and limits

Every tracked file in scope was read line by line on 2026-11-11; numeric tables were recomputed with exact decimal arithmetic, and each official URL was fetched live the same day. The audit is read-only: it reports, it does not repair other sessions’ files. A source link beside a finding identifies where a human reviewer can confirm the underlying rule; it does not certify correctness of a repo file that this session did not author.

Research only - not legal advice, not a rent-increase notice, and not a certification of any unit. No inventory filing, license request, tenant notice or attorney engagement was performed.

Privacy first: placeholder identities only (Unit A / Unit B; Tenant A1, Tenant A4, Tenant B1, Tenant B2). No tenant names, initials, street or unit addresses, parcel numbers or contact details are published. Dates and dollar amounts are reproduced exactly as supplied by the owner; the combination can still permit re-identification, so treat this page as sensitive.
