How large corporate owners and individual owners handle increases

Verified: the legal duties are identical for both - the same ordinance sections bind every owner of a covered unit (S02). Not verified: no official empirical study describing how the two owner types actually choose increase amounts was found; that comparison value stays blank (FR-11).

Raise one unit or both?

No uniform-increase statute was identified in the reviewed sources: nothing requires the same percentage on every unit. The risk lives in motive and pattern, not in arithmetic.

Twelve-point tenant-lawyer stress test

This mirrors what free tenant-side review channels in San Francisco would check. A tenant can file a summary petition (Form 516C) at no cost with no filing deadline (S17); eviction defense carries a right-to-counsel referral under Form 1007 (S18). Treat every point as pass/fail with evidence, not as a vibe.
