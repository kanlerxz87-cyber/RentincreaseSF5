The nine-step compliance path

Numbered in the order a first-time owner should work through them. Steps 01-04 are prerequisites; steps 05-08 decide the amount and notice; step 09 preserves the evidence a reviewer would request.

What the Housing Inventory report asks for

For a unit that is not owner-occupied, Sec. 37.15(b)(2) lists the exact fields below. Owner-occupied units report only the address and owner-occupied status. One co-owner can file for all owners of a unit.

Section 37.15(b)(2) reporting fields - tenant-occupied units

Rent Board Fee calendar - separate from the license

The annual fee funds the Rent Board; it is independent of Housing Inventory reporting and the rent-increase license. Requesting a fee exemption does not satisfy inventory reporting.
