## Bottom line

The consolidated dashboard is suitable for publication as a research record, not as approval of a specific notice.
The headline answer is confirmed: an individual owner must report each unit into the Housing Inventory; reporting
automatically generates the rent-increase licence for a non-owner-occupied unit. The licence must be current on the
increase's effective date. There is no separate licence application or ordinary annual/banked-increase petition.
Sources: [Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory),
[§37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information--sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing), and
[§37.3](https://www.sf.gov/information--sec-373-rent-limitations).
