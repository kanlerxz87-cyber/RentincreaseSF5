Source register

Every source is a government publication: the City and County of San Francisco (including Rent Board forms and the Chapter 37 ordinance PDF), the California Legislative Information service, HUD, HUD USER and SF Open Data. All were retrieved and read on November 7, 2026.

Excluded on purpose. Law-firm blogs, property-management marketing pages, rent-calculator sites and forum posts appeared in search results and were not used to support any row. Where they made a claim this project could not confirm - such as a licence "application" - the correction is recorded on the Flagged for Review page instead.

S33SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Failure to Repair and Maintain Petitions (Form 516B)
Agency guidance page		https://www.sf.gov/information--failure-repair-and-maintain-petitions/A tenant may petition to DEFER an annual rent increase where the landlord has not made repairs required by state or local law; the petition must be filed no later than 60 days after receipt of the rent increase notice; if granted the increase is deferred until repairs are made and the landlord must refund any increase paid since the effective date; the petition will not be granted if repairs were completed before the effective date; the anniversary date is unaffected.
