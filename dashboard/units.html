Calculation

  • U1-A: 1.6% (Mar 1, 2026 - Feb 28, 2027) = 1.6%. $3,995.00 × 1.6% = $63.92, so the new rent is $4,058.92. Notice period: 30 days.
    Most conservative option. Uses only the rate published for the effective date and ignores the skipped 2026 anniversary.
  • U1-B: 1.4% (Mar 1, 2025 - Feb 28, 2026) + 1.6% (Mar 1, 2026 - Feb 28, 2027) = 3%. $3,995.00 × 3% = $119.85, so the new rent is $4,114.85. Notice period: 30 days.
    Treats the missed February 1, 2026 increase as banked under SF Admin Code §37.3(a)(2). See flagged item FL-03: sf.gov's banking page describes a 24-month condition that the ordinance text does not contain, so this option should be confirmed with a Rent Board counsellor before a notice is served.

Calculation

  • U2-A: 1.6% (Mar 1, 2026 - Feb 28, 2027) = 1.6%. $3,150.00 × 1.6% = $50.40, so the new rent is $3,200.40. Notice period: 30 days.
    Lowest-risk option; leaves roughly six years of banked increases unused. Notice period: 30 days.
  • U2-B: 1.8% (Mar 1, 2020 - Feb 28, 2021) + 0.7% (Mar 1, 2021 - Feb 28, 2022) + 2.3% (Mar 1, 2022 - Feb 28, 2023) + 3.6% (Mar 1, 2023 - Feb 29, 2024) + 1.7% (Mar 1, 2024 - Feb 28, 2025) + 1.4% (Mar 1, 2025 - Feb 28, 2026) + 1.6% (Mar 1, 2026 - Feb 28, 2027) = 13.1%. $3,150.00 × 13.1% = $412.65, so the new rent is $3,562.65. Notice period: 90 days.
    Legally available under §37.3(a)(2) and the Rent Board's multiple-years guidance, but the total exceeds 10%, which triggers a 90-day notice, and a six-year catch-up is the classic trigger for a tenant petition. Licensed and permitted, but the highest-scrutiny option.
  • U2-C: 1.6% (Mar 1, 2026 - Feb 28, 2027) = 10%. $3,150.00 × 10% = $315.00, so the new rent is $3,465.00. Notice period: 30 days.
    A policy choice, not a legal requirement: 10% is the notice-period threshold in Civil Code §827 and the emergency price-gouging ceiling in Penal Code §396, so staying at or under it keeps the increase in the 30-day notice band and below the gouging line if an emergency were declared.

Cross-cutting notes

Unit 1 changed occupants. Two rules bite, and neither allows a market-rate reset now. Costa-Hawkins §1954.53(d)(3) says the market-rate right does not apply to a partial change of occupancy where one of the original occupants remains - and one does. Rules & Regs §6.14 permits an unlimited increase when the last original occupant vacates, but only if the new occupant was served a written 6.14 notice within a reasonable time of moving in, generally 60 days. Whether that happened is unknown (FL-05) and it is worth checking, because it is the difference between a controlled rent and a market rent later.

Unit 2's target date matters. The 1.6% rate is published only through February 28, 2027. An increase effective after that date needs the rate that will not be published until March 1, 2027, which nobody can verify today. Because Unit 2's full entitlement exceeds 10%, a 90-day notice is required - which for a February 28, 2027 effective date means service by Nov 30, 2026 (Nov 25, 2026 if mailed). Those dates are close.
