Everything in square brackets is yours to complete accurately. Delete the lines that do not apply to the unit you are serving.</p><pre class="notice">NOTICE OF RENT INCREASE

Date of this notice: [date you deliver or mail it]
To: [tenant name or names as they appear on the rental agreement]
Rental unit: [unit identifier, not the full street address]
Current base rent: $[amount] per month
New base rent: $[amount] per month
Amount of the increase: $[amount] per month
Percentage increase: [x.x]% of the current base rent
Effective date: [a date at least 30 days after this notice, or at least 90 days if the increase is more than 10 percent; add 5 days if this notice is mailed]

How the increase is made up (Rent Ordinance sec. 37.3(b); Rules and Regulations sec. 4.10(a)):
  - Annual allowable increase: [x.x]%, being the Rent Board allowance in effect on the effective date
  - Banked increase: [x.x]%, based on the rent increase anniversary dates of [list the dates]

Rent increase licence: this unit is reported in the Rent Board Housing Inventory and a rent increase
licence is on file. The licence does not need to be attached to this notice.

Questions: Rent Board counsellors, 415-252-4600, weekdays 9am to noon and 1pm to 4pm.

Your options: you may ask the Rent Board to review this increase by filing Tenant Petition Form 516C
(there is no filing fee and no time limit). If repairs you have requested in writing have not been
made, you may file Form 516B within 60 days of receiving this notice; a granted petition postpones
the increase until the work is done.

Served by: [personal delivery / first-class mail] on [date]</pre></section><section class="panel"><h2>Before you serve: the checklist</h2><ul class="tick"><li>Both units are reported in the Housing Inventory for the current reporting year, and the licence shows in the Rent Board Portal.</li><li>The anniversary date is confirmed from the rent ledger - for Unit 1 that turns on whether any original lessee remained (F20).</li><li>At least 12 months have passed since the last increase on that tenancy.</li><li>The percentage used is the one in effect on the effective date: 1.6% for any effective date up to 28 February 2027.</li><li>Only properly banked amounts are added, nothing is compounded, and nothing is rounded up.</li><li>The notice states the dollar amount, the percentage, the effective date, the annual portion and the banked portion with its dates.</li><li>The notice period matches the size of the increase: 30 days at or below 10%, 90 days above 10%, plus 5 days if mailed.</li><li>No increase is charged because a new occupant moved in, and no passthrough has been folded into base rent.</li><li>There are no outstanding written repair requests on the unit that could support a Form 516B petition.</li><li>A short written note explains, per unit, the business basis for the increase and why the two units differ. Keep it in the file.</li><li>A copy of the notice and proof of service are kept with the lease and the rent ledger.</li></ul></section><section class="panel"><h2>The three ways a notice fails</h2><div class="tw"><table><caption>The failures that void or defer an increase</caption><thead><tr><th>Failure</th><th>Consequence</th><th>Status and source</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Served without a current licence</td><td>The notice is not enforceable for the months of suspension, and reporting later does not revive them.</td><td><span class="badge ok">Verified</span> <a class="src" href="https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory" target="_blank" rel="noopener" title="SF Rent Board -
