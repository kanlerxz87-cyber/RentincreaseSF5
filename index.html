The license question, in one paragraph

Yes — as an individual (non-corporate) owner you still must have a rent increase license. San Francisco requires all owners of residential property to report tenant-occupied units into the Rent Board Housing Inventory by March 1 each year; a rent increase license is automatically generated when a tenant-occupied unit is reported. Without a current license on the effective date of the increase, the increase is not enforceable. There is no separate application or approval step, and the rule makes no distinction between individual and corporate owners. Full answer with official links: Executive Summary →
