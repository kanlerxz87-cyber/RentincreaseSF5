The question you asked first

Do I have to inform the Rent Board and apply for a licence before notifying tenants of a rent increase, even as an individual owner (not a corporation)?

Yes to the licence — no to an application for approval. Every owner of a San Francisco residential unit, individual or corporate, must report into the Rent Board's Housing Inventory; doing so generates a rent increase licence for a tenant-occupied unit, and that licence must already be current on the effective date of the increase notice or the increase is not enforceable S3 S4. But there is no application and no Rent Board approval for an ordinary annual or banked increase — the ordinance gives you a licence to impose it S5. Only the six special increase types (capital improvement, operating & maintenance, comparables, Prop I history, utility passthrough, §1.21) require a petition filed before the notice S19.
