Your first question: the Rent Board and the licence

Yes, you need the licence. No, you do not apply for it, and being an individual owner changes nothing.

Section 37.15(a) obliges every owner of a residential unit covered by the Rent Ordinance to report into the Rent Board's Housing Inventory. There is no corporation threshold and no small-owner exemption. Once a unit is reported as tenant-occupied, a rent increase licence is generated automatically - there is no application form and no approval to wait for. S1 S2

The licence must already be on file on the effective date of the increase. If it is not, the licence is suspended, the notice is not enforceable, and the tenant owes no increased rent for the months of suspension. Reporting later restores the licence prospectively only. S2

You do not need a petition or Board approval for an ordinary annual or banked increase. Petitions are only for the special increase types such as certified capital improvements. S3 S13

So the order is: report into the Inventory first, confirm the licence is on file, then serve the notice. Read the nine-step process →

Does the state 8.8% cap or any federal law apply?The AB 1482 cap does not replace a stricter applicable local limit (Civil Code sec. 1947.12(d)(3)), and no federal rent ceiling was identified. Coverage of these units still needs confirming.Law and protectionsS9 S10 S19
How do big operators and individual owners differ?Not in the cap - the same 1.6% applies to any landlord. What differs is the recommended process: a compliance calendar, a per-unit ledger and documented calculations. No official study of actual industry practice was confirmed. Algorithmic rent-setting on non-public competitor data is unlawful in San Francisco.Owner practicesS3 S16
How should tenants be told, and what reasons are reasonable?A written notice stating the dollar amount, the percentage, the effective date and any banked portion: 30 days at or below 10% and 90 days above, plus 5 days if mailed. No reason is required, but the reason must not be retaliatory, discriminatory or coercive.NoticesS8 S7 S15
