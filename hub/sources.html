Method and limits

Each page listed above was opened and read, not merely search-listed. Quotations in the register are taken from the text of those pages. Arithmetic is computed from the published percentages and the owner-supplied base rents and is labelled computed. Where an official page could not be reached, or a value is not published, the cell is left blank and the item appears on the flagged page.

Two limits are worth stating plainly. First, external links are checked for shape and publisher at build time, not for perpetual availability; government sites move. Second, no property record, portal account, lease or rent ledger was available, so nothing here certifies that a particular increase on a particular unit is lawful. It sets out the rules, the arithmetic and the evidence you would need.
