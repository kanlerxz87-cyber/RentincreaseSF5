Notices

The notice is where most increases fail. The period, the content and the method of service are all prescribed.

Reasons for the increase

An annual or banked increase does not need a stated justification - the ordinance authorises it and the notice must instead identify the annual and banked portions. S3 S7 Reasons and cost evidence belong to petition-based increases such as certified capital improvements. S3 In practice a short, factual covering note helps: it should refer to the published annual percentage and the anniversary date, and should avoid any reference to a tenant's personal circumstances, a request for repairs, or a wish to see the unit vacated. Those are the facts that turn a lawful number into a retaliation or harassment claim. S17 S15
